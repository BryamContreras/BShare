BShare helps you send files, apps, photos, videos, folders, and screen shares between nearby devices on your local network.

Features:
- Send files directly over LAN or a temporary BShare Wi-Fi network.
- Scan a QR code to connect nearby devices.
- Share your screen in real time.
- Receive files into a BShare folder on your device.
- Works across Android, Windows, Linux, and other supported desktop builds.

BShare is designed for local transfer workflows. Files are sent directly between your devices and are not uploaded to a BShare cloud service.
